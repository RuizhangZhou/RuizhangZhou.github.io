02 / HOW I WORK

Care is in the details.

Good engineering is a sequence of decisions you can explain, test and stand behind.

01.

Start with the real problem.

What does someone need to do? What gets in their way? I want the answer before choosing the model, framework or abstraction.

02.

Make failure understandable.

A timeout, a broken stream and a wrong usage count are different problems. I trace the path, reproduce what I can and keep assumptions visible.

03.

Leave something others can use.

Readable code, clear explanations and honest boundaries matter. I care about the person using the software and the person maintaining it next.
